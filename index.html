Our Story

When we opened our doors on Broadway, we wanted to share more than just our favorite recipes. We wanted to share our childhood memories of Thailand.

The word Pye (พาย) means "paddle." It honors the street vendors who rowed small wooden boats along Bangkok's busy canals, serving hot bowls of noodles to neighbors right from the water. Growing up, that lively energy and the rich aroma of simmering herbs defined comfort for us.

We built this restaurant to recreate that timeless feeling right here in Queens. Every morning, we slow-simmer our signature dark broths, hand-crush our spices, and prepare our noodles exactly the way our families did generations ago.

Whether you are joining us in our nostalgic dining room or relaxing in our backyard garden, you are a guest in our home. Welcome to our table.
